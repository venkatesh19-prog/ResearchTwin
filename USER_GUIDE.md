# ResearchTwin User Guide

Open https://starlit-empanada-a323e3.netlify.app/researchtwin.html.

**Signing in**
- Sign in with your email and password, or use **Create account** to register as a student or faculty member. Administrator accounts are created by the operator.
- Your session lasts up to 12 hours in that browser tab. **Sign out** (bottom of the sidebar) ends it on the server.
- The sidebar scrolls on its own. On small screens it becomes a wrapped menu at the top.
- The free hosting plan sleeps when idle, so the first action after a pause can take up to a minute.

## Students

| Sidebar entry | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **ML Recommendations** | Get ranked researcher matches with an explanation of each score. Save your profile first. |
| **Student Profile** | Record research areas, expertise, skills, projects, publications, courses and academic details. |
| **Researcher Directory** | Search and filter researchers; open a profile; view a skill-gap roadmap and tick off milestones. |
| **My Requests** | Track collaboration requests: pending, accepted or rejected, with the faculty response. |
| **Opportunities** | Browse research and funding opportunities, check your eligibility, and apply. |
| **My Applications** | Follow application status and withdraw a pending application. |
| **Research Teams** | Create a team, invite members, accept invitations, and get collaborator recommendations. |
| **Workspaces** | Each accepted collaboration opens a workspace with tasks, milestones and comments. |
| **Research Labs / Institutions** | See your institution and the labs visible to you. |
| **Inter-university Collaborations** | View collaborations of labs you belong to. |
| **Literature Library** | Keep your own reading list with notes, tags and review status. |
| **Research Proposals** | Write a proposal in sections, invite editors or viewers, cite your literature, and save versions. |
| **Thesis Tracking** | Record your thesis with a faculty supervisor, add milestones, move it through its stages. |
| **Publications** | Track a submission from draft to publication, with revisions. |
| **Analytics / Research Intelligence** | Explore university statistics, research clusters, trends, the similarity network and topic prediction. |
| **Search History** | Review your recent searches. |

## Faculty

- **Get started:** fill in your **Faculty Profile**. An administrator links your account to your directory record; after that you can submit a **Researcher Verification** request.
- **Collaboration Requests:** accept or reject student requests. Accepting opens a shared workspace.
- **Opportunities:** publish research opportunities for your linked researcher record and review applicants.
- **Research Labs:** create labs in your institution, choose public or institution-only visibility, and add members.
- **Inter-university Collaborations:** propose a collaboration with a lab at another institution, or respond to one.
- **Thesis Tracking:** follow theses you supervise.
- **Also available:** Research Teams, Workspaces, Literature, Proposals, Publications, Analytics and Research Intelligence work as they do for students.

## Administrators

- **Account Management:**
  - list and filter accounts;
  - change roles and activate or deactivate accounts;
  - revoke sessions and reset passwords;
  - export an account's data;
  - manage faculty-to-researcher links.
- **Groups:** groups are role cohorts. Students belong to `students` and faculty to `faculty`, automatically on registration. You can remove a member and add them back to their own role's group.
- **Researcher Directory:** add, edit, delete or import researchers.
- **Researcher Verification:** review pending requests and verify or reject them.
- **Institutions:** create institutions and assign members.
- **Opportunities:** publish funding opportunities.
- **Research Intelligence:** rebuild research clusters.
- **Audit Log:** review authentication and administrative events.
